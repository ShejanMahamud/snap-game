                  SNAP: a free rubber-band gem slicing game

                  SNAP is a quick arcade game you play in your browser, on a phone or a computer. Pull the rubber band down, let go, and the band flies up and slices the gems it touches. It is free, needs no sign-up, and works offline once it has loaded.

                  How to play

                  • Pull: drag down anywhere. A longer pull snaps harder.
                  • Release: the band flies up and slices whatever it touches.
                  • Chain: slice many gems in one snap to grow your combo.
                  • Fever: fill the pink bar for six seconds of double points.

                  Controls

                  Touch or mouse: drag down and release. Keyboard: Space or Down arrow to pull, Left and Right to aim, P to pause.

                  Modes

                  • Daily: everyone plays the same seed each day, with a daily leaderboard.
                  • Endless: survive as long as you can. Weekly and all-time leaderboards.
                  • Hard: a tougher endless run, unlocked at level 10.
                  • Stages: 200 campaign stages across 20 worlds, with bosses and three stars each.

                  Earn coins to unlock band skins and stage boosts in the shop. SNAP can be installed on a phone or computer like an app.
